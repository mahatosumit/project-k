For a customer of a business using {{ app_name }}

The fastest route is the grievance link on the payment page you were sent. It reaches that business with a tracking reference, and it is the same path to use for a data request such as correcting or deleting your information. You will get an acknowledgement with a reference number, and a response within {{ complaint_window_hours }} hours.

That business is the data fiduciary for your information. {{ app_name }} is the software it uses, so requests about your data are normally answered by them, not by us.

For a business using {{ app_name }}

Write to {{ support_email }} for anything to do with the service, including billing, refunds, security questions, and difficulty connecting a payment gateway. Please include the invoice or order reference where you have one — it makes the request much faster to act on.

Security incidents

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, or you believe your account has been accessed without permission, write to {{ support_email }} with the details and do not test the issue against other users' data. Reports are treated as urgent, and internet-facing incidents are reported to CERT-In within {{ cert_in_hours }} hours as the directions require.

Escalation

  1. Raise the issue through the in-app support or the grievance form, so it gets a reference.
  2. If it is not resolved, ask for it to be escalated to the grievance officer in writing.
  3. If it is still not resolved, the next step under Indian law depends on the nature of the complaint — for data matters it is the Data Protection Board of India, and for consumer matters the consumer forum. We will tell you clearly which applies rather than leaving you to work it out.
