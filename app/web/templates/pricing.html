Do you send messages to my customers without asking me?

No. You see every scheduled message before it goes, you can change the schedule, and we only send on WhatsApp to customers who have agreed to it. If a customer replies with a dispute, a payment claim, or a request for a plan, the reminders stop immediately and we tell you.

Is this billing software?

No. Use whatever you already use to raise invoices if you like — this is about what happens after the invoice goes out, which is where money actually gets stuck. If you do want GST invoices from here, they are generated with the buyer's GSTIN, place of supply and the correct CGST/SGST or IGST split.
