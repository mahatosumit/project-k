These terms describe the service as it actually behaves. They have not been reviewed by a lawyer yet; that review is a required action in the project's HUMAN_TODO before onboarding paying customers.

1. What the service is

{{ app_name }} helps a business record invoices and follow up on unpaid ones: it schedules and sends reminders over messaging channels, lets the customer pay through the business's own payment gateway, records what happened, and produces documents for late payment. It is a tool for collecting money you are owed. It is not a lawyer, an accountant, or a collections agency.

2. What it is not

  • It is not a payment aggregator. Money moves through the gateway the business connects, and {{ app_name }} never holds, routes, or controls those funds.
  • It does not give tax, legal, or financial advice. GST figures come from the details you enter and the rate you choose; your accountant remains responsible for your returns and your invoices.
  • It does not guarantee that any invoice will be paid, and nothing in it constitutes a threat, a notice, or a legal proceeding on your behalf.
  • The MSMED interest figure is a described calculation, not advice, and it stays blank unless the current RBI bank rate is configured. Confirm it with a professional before you rely on it.

3. Your responsibilities as a business using it

  • Only message people you have a genuine commercial relationship with, and only after they have agreed to receive messages on that channel. You are the one who holds the consent; the product records it and refuses to send without it.
  • Enter accurate invoice data. A wrong amount or a wrong GSTIN on an invoice is a dispute you created, not a software bug.
  • Do not use the service to harass anyone, to send marketing, or to send anything you would not be willing to show a customer.
  • Keep your own credentials to your payment gateway safe. You connect your own gateway, so your money and your gateway relationship are your own.
  • Follow Indian law that applies to you, including tax, data protection, and telecom rules.

4. Accounts and security

You are responsible for keeping access to your account secure. Passwords must meet the stated minimum, sessions are invalidated when a password changes, and failed sign-ins are rate limited and may lock an account briefly. Tell us at once if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

5. Fees and payment

Subscription prices are shown in Indian rupees and are exclusive of GST where GST applies. We charge for a period up front rather than taking an automatic debit, so you always know what is being charged and when. A free trial is offered without a card. If a renewal payment is not made, the service moves to a limited state: your data stays, reminders pause, and nothing is deleted for 90 days. Refunds are governed by the {{ t('legal.refund') }}.

6. Messaging costs and limits

Reminder messages are sent through the business's configured provider, and any per-message cost charged by that provider is borne by the business. WhatsApp messages are subject to that platform's own template and opt-in rules, and SMS requires the sender to be registered under TRAI's framework. Where registration is missing, the product will refuse to send rather than send something that will be blocked.

7. Data

Your data is yours. We process it as described in the privacy policy. You can export or delete a customer's data from inside the product. If you close your account, you can take an export with you; statutory retention duties may keep some records longer, and we will tell you which.

8. Availability and limits of liability

We aim to keep the service running and will maintain and back it up, but it is offered as is. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for lost profits, and our total liability is limited to the subscription fees you paid in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited under Indian law.

9. Suspension and termination

You can stop using the service at any time and cancel renewals with one click. We may suspend an account that is being used to harass people, to send unlawful content, or in a way that puts the service or other users at risk — and we will tell you why.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at the place of business of the operator of this deployment have jurisdiction.
