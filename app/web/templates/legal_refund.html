Free trial

New accounts get 14 days with everything switched on and no card required. Nothing is charged during the trial, so there is nothing to refund if you decide not to continue.

Subscription refunds

  • Within 14 days of a charge: if you have not used the paid period to send reminders for more than a handful of invoices, write to us and we will refund the period in full. No arguments.
  • After 14 days: the remaining unused full months are refunded on a pro-rata basis for annual plans. For monthly plans we do not refund a partly used month, but we will stop the renewal so you are not charged again.
  • Annual plan price advantage: if you cancel an annual plan after the first 14 days, the months already used are recalculated at the monthly rate, and the difference is deducted from the refund. This is only so that cancelling early is not cheaper than having taken the monthly plan.
  • Our fault: if we charged you in error, or the service was unusable for a sustained period and we could not fix it, the refund is full regardless of the above.

How to ask

There is a refund request form on the billing page, so you do not have to find an email address. It creates a tracked request. You will get an acknowledgement, and we aim to decide within 48 hours. Refunds are made to the original payment method through the payment gateway, and the time the money takes to appear is set by your bank or UPI provider, typically 5–7 working days after we process it.

What is not refunded

  • Message costs charged to you directly by your WhatsApp or SMS provider. Those are paid to that provider, not to us, and we cannot refund them.
  • Fees charged by your payment gateway on the payments your own customers made to you. That money never came through us.

Cancelling

Cancelling is one click from the billing page and takes effect for the next renewal. It never takes away a period you have already paid for. Your data stays available for 90 days after the paid period ends, and you can export it at any time in that window.

Complaints

If a refund decision does not satisfy you, escalate it to the grievance officer listed in the {{ t('legal.contact') }} section. We will respond in writing with reasons.
