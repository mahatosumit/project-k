# 06 — Go-to-market

Constraint accepted up front: this is a solo operation with no paid acquisition
budget. Every channel below is one a person can work by hand, in the order that is
actually walkable.

---

## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

> Vasool chases your unpaid invoices on WhatsApp in Hindi or English — politely,
> on a schedule you approve — and hands you the paperwork if the customer still
> will not pay.

**Primary segment:** service firms and agencies, 10–200 staff, ₹5–50 lakh
receivables, at least some of it more than 45 days old. Delhi NCR, Pune,
Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Indore, Jaipur.

**Who it is not for, said plainly on the site:** kirana and micro-retail (they
will not pay this price), anyone wanting credit or lending, anyone wanting tax
advice.

## 5. WhatsApp onboarding and support flow

Support over WhatsApp is the norm in this market, so the product meets people
there rather than asking them to use a portal.

**Outbound sequence after signup (each step a real sent message, not an e-mail):**

1. **Immediately:** "Your account is ready. Add your first invoice here: {link}. You will see all 9 reminder messages before anything is sent."
2. **+1 hour, only if no invoice yet:** "Stuck? Send me the customer name and amount and I will set the first invoice up for you." (Offering to do it for them converts far better than documentation.)
3. **+1 day, if an invoice exists but no gateway:** "Invoices are ready. Connect Razorpay or Cashfree so customers can pay you directly — 3 minutes here: {link}. I can walk you through it on a call."
4. **+3 days:** "Want me to look at your reminder wording? Reply with the invoice number."
5. **Day 13 (before trial end):** "Your trial ends tomorrow. Your options: ₹299 for a month, or ₹2,999 for a year (two months free). Either way your data stays for 90 days."

**Support rules:** answer within 4 working hours; never send a template answer;
if two merchants ask the same thing, it is a product gap, not a support gap.

## 6. What we will not do

* Buy a list, or message people who have not asked.
* Post a link where the group rules forbid it.
* Fabricate a review, a customer count, or a logo.
* Claim MSMED interest as a certainty: the number comes from a rate the user must
  verify, and we say so.
* Promise collection. We promise the follow-up; the customer still has to pay.
